# Luna

Luna is the current player avatar and emotional center of the prototype. Mechanically, she reads as a small, frightened survivor who converts comfort and caution into staying power, then occasionally cashes that safety into a decisive attack.

## Current Combat Identity

- Combat nameplate: `The Little Dog`
- Avatar nameplate: `Luna`
- Starting HP: `28`
- Base courage per turn: `3`
- Starting hand target: `5`
- Starter deck size: `18`

## Mechanical Themes

- Bracing and surviving through block
- Comfort objects as persistent buffs
- A small amount of self-healing
- Courage as both action economy and emotional framing
- One all-in finisher in `Bite`

## Tonal Role

- The text treats fear as a moment-to-moment experience rather than a character flaw.
- Card names and flavor text keep the tone tender and intimate.
- The current framing suggests a game about moving through fear, not erasing it.
